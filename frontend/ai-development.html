The opportunity

Start with the use case. Build around the evidence.

AI works best when the task, available data and success criteria are clear. We help your team assess opportunities and choose an approach that fits the product and its operating constraints.

Do we need to train a model from scratch?

Not always. Existing model APIs, retrieval approaches or smaller task-specific models may be suitable. We evaluate options against the use case and available data.

Can AI use our internal knowledge?

We can design access-aware knowledge retrieval and integrations with your information sources, subject to the data and permission requirements agreed for the project.

How do you assess output quality?

We define representative evaluation cases and review quality, limitations and failure behavior before release. Human review is included where the workflow requires it.
