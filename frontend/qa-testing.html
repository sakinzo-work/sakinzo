The opportunity

Find the risks that matter to your users.

A useful test strategy starts with understanding how the product is used and where failure would cause the most harm. We combine targeted manual testing and automation to give your team actionable release evidence.

We can work alongside development or assess a product preparing for launch, covering core journeys, integrations, compatibility and performance within an agreed scope.

Can you test a product built by another team?

Yes. We agree access to the application, requirements and test environments, then define the assessment scope with your team.

Will every test be automated?

No. Automation is useful for repeatable checks; exploratory and usability-related testing still need human judgment. The mix follows the product risks.

Does a passed test suite mean there are no defects?

No test process can prove a product is defect-free. We report what was tested, what was found and the limitations of the assessment.
