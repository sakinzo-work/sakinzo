Can you work with our existing application?

Yes. We can assess the current codebase, identify integration points and plan improvements alongside your team. The approach depends on the architecture and condition of the product.

Do we need separate web and mobile apps?

We help choose between a responsive website, a native application and a cross-platform app based on device needs, user behavior, delivery priorities and maintenance.

What happens after launch?

We can plan maintenance, monitoring and future iterations with your team. Support scope and responsibilities are agreed as part of the engagement.
