The opportunity

Make the important decisions before they become expensive.

Architecture is a set of choices about how a product will work, change and be operated. We make those choices explicit, using your requirements and constraints to guide the design.

Whether you are planning a new platform or reviewing a system that has grown over time, we examine boundaries, data flows, integrations and operational risks to create a practical path forward.

Do you only design new systems?

No. We can review an existing architecture, plan an integration or help a team modernize part of a platform.

Will you recommend a particular technology stack?

Recommendations follow the product requirements, team experience, existing systems and operating constraints. We document the reasons and tradeoffs.

Can our own engineers implement the architecture?

Yes. Deliverables can include diagrams, decision records and implementation guidance for your internal engineering team.
