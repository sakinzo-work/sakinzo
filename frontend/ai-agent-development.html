The opportunity

Connect intelligence to action, with boundaries.

An agent needs more than a prompt. It needs a defined job, access to the right information and tools, and clear rules for when to ask a person to decide.

We map the workflow, build integrations and evaluate behavior across realistic scenarios so your team can understand what the system can do and how it should be operated.

Can the agent take actions in our tools?

Yes, where integrations and permissions support it. We define allowed actions and approval requirements with your team before enabling them.

Can people review important decisions?

Yes. Human review and approval points can be built into the workflow for actions that require oversight.

How is an agent different from a chatbot?

A chatbot primarily handles a conversation. An agent can carry out a defined multi-step task using tools and context; the required level of autonomy depends on the workflow.
