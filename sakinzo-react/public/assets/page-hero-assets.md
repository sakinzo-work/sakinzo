# Public page hero images

Six backgrounds were created with the built-in imagegen tool, then encoded as WebP at original dimensions and quality 92. The people are fictional illustrative professionals, not photographs of Sakinzo staff.

The page-to-image mapping is in `frontend/page-covers.js`. Services retain their existing cover catalog; Home retains its branded network-wave artwork. Blog articles and projects use their uploaded cover first, with a category-specific local fallback. Existing service image sources are in `service-hero-assets.md`.

## Saved assets and final prompts

### page-company.webp

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: website hero background photograph, wide panoramic 3:1 composition approximately 2160 by 720. Primary request: A small diverse software product team collaborating around a laptop in a contemporary office, realistic candid work, subtle diagram screen and glass meeting room, no staged handshake. Style: premium photorealistic cinematic image, deep navy and restrained electric blue with teal accents matching a software engineering website. Composition: all important subjects entirely within the RIGHT 45 percent, with generous top and side margins; LEFT 55 percent is uncluttered dark navy negative space for large white headings. Lighting is soft and credible, detailed natural materials, calm professional mood. One continuous photograph, not a website screenshot or collage. No logos, brand names, watermark, large type or readable text. People, where present, are generic fictional professionals, not actual company staff.
